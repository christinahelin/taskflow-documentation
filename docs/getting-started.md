# Getting Started with TaskFlow

TaskFlow is a project management application that helps teams organize projects, assign tasks, track deadlines, and monitor progress.

## Before You Begin

To use TaskFlow, you need an active user account and access to your organization's TaskFlow workspace. Your available features may vary depending on your assigned role.

TaskFlow includes three user roles:

- **Team Member:** View assigned tasks, update task status, add comments, and complete tasks.
- **Project Manager:** Create and manage projects, assign tasks, set deadlines and priorities, and monitor project progress.
- **Administrator:** Manage users, roles, permissions, and workspace settings.

## Sign In to TaskFlow

1. Open TaskFlow in your web browser.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Sign In**.

## Navigate the Dashboard

After you sign in, the TaskFlow dashboard provides an overview of your work. The information displayed depends on your assigned role.

- **Team Members** can view assigned tasks, upcoming deadlines, and task status.
- **Project Managers** can view project progress, outstanding tasks, overdue work, and team assignments.
- **Administrators** can access user management, permissions, workspace settings, and usage information.
